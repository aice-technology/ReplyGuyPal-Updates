Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an OpenAI account?

Yes, you'll need your own OpenAI API key. This ensures your data stays private and you have full control. API keys are free to create at platform.openai.com.

How does the CSV import work?

Export your X (Twitter) post history as a CSV, then upload it to ReplyGuyPal. The app analyzes your writing style, tone, and personality to generate suggestions that sound authentically like you.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. ReplyGuyPal stores your OpenAI API key securely in macOS Keychain. Your data never touches our servers. Everything is processed locally on your Mac and sent directly to OpenAI using your own API key.

What platforms do you support?

Currently, ReplyGuyPal works with X (Twitter). We're actively developing support for LinkedIn, Instagram, and other platforms. A Chrome extension is also in the works!

Can I get a refund?

Since ReplyGuyPal costs just £1, we don't offer refunds. However, we're committed to making the app amazing. If you have issues or feedback, please reach out to us on GitHub!
